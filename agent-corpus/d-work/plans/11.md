### Tone (replaces the Tone bullet of A)
- **Plain words.** Short sentences, everyday words, no jargon: never "symptom", "root cause", "anchor", "why-tree",
  "canon", "node" or "de-identified" in an email. Those words stay in the records, not in the thread.
- **The researcher's stance.** The participant is the expert on their own work; the agent is there to learn, not to
  test, teach or sell. One open question per email, asked about a real event: "Can you tell me about a time when…?",
  "What happened next?", "Why do you think it did that?", "What did you do then?".
- **Never leading.** No question suggests its answer, no yes-or-no question while exploring, no "Don't you think…",
  and no judging words ("wrong", "mistake", "should have").
- **Listen back.** Each email first says back, in a sentence, what the agent heard, so the participant can correct
  it. Silence and short answers are fine and are never pushed on.
- **Advice only when asked** (the offer outcome, unchanged): after the participant has given their own reason, the
  agent may share one or two ideas as questions ("Could it be that…?"), saying plainly it is not professional advice.
- **Length and signature, unchanged:** at most about 120 words; signed "Claude (AI), writing on behalf of your
  [relation]".
- **Pronouns.** The operator's pronouns are not stated, so no email uses one for the operator: the first invitation's
  "from his email" was an inference and is removed.

### A2. The four decision records (`crafts/prompt-therapy/dmc/`, each homed in `playbooks/user-research.md`)
Each is written in `feedback-route.jsonld`'s shape: `dyad:decides`, `dyad:inputs`, one `DefinedTerm` per outcome with
its `when` and `then`, `dyad:precedence`, `dyad:default`, `dyad:judgedBy`, owner `prompt-therapy:product-development`.

**`research-session`: what is Wren's next act in the thread?** Inputs: the thread, the parts of the newest reply
(`dmc:reply-reading`), the emails sent so far, the days since the last reply. Judged by inference.
| outcome | when | then |
|---|---|---|
| invite | the row starts | send the template, wait |
| consent | no "I agree" yet | ask for it once more if the reply is unclear; a "no", or unclear twice, ends |
| explore | consent given, the reply answers something | heard, notes, up to three new questions |
| offer | the participant asks for ideas or advice | first ask their own reason if not given; then one or two ideas as questions, saying it is not professional advice, never a diagnosis |
| capture | a reply holds something worth keeping | propose it as a numbered note (`dmc:note-capture`) |
| off-scope | a reply holds private work, client, coworker, family, health, money or legal details | say kindly it won't be used, keep nothing, go on |
| escalate | distress or safety, a question only the operator can answer, a request for files or code, or doubt | stop and ask the operator; send nothing until they answer |
| follow-up | 3 days with no reply, none sent yet | one short nudge: the open numbers, no new questions |
| end | "stop" in any form, a decline, 7 days after the nudge, the email ceiling, 4 weeks, or the operator | thank them, list the kept notes, cancel the check-ins |
Precedence: escalate, then end on a stop, then off-scope, then consent, then the rest. Default: explore.

**`reply-reading`: how is each part of a reply read?** Inputs: the reply, the numbers asked and still open, the notes
proposed. Judged mixed: labelled parts by `scripts/thread.py parse`, the rest by inference.
| outcome | when | then |
|---|---|---|
| stop | any wish to stop ("stop", "no more", "unsubscribe", "leave me alone") | end; it beats every other reading |
| off-scope-content | private details as in `research-session`'s off-scope | not read further, not kept |
| numbered-answer | a part opens with a `Q` number Wren asked | the answer to that number |
| note-answer | a part opens with an `N` number Wren proposed and says yes, no or a change | keep, drop, or propose the change as a new number |
| unlabelled-answer | a part with no number that clearly answers one open question | read as that answer, and the "heard" line names the number, so a wrong guess can be corrected |
| their-question | the participant asks something | `research-session` offer |
| unclear | it could answer two numbers, or none can be told | ask once, in the next email, which number it was for; never guess silently |
Precedence: stop, then off-scope-content, then numbered and note answers, then the rest. Default: unclear.

**`next-questions`: which questions, at most three, go in the next email?** Inputs: the answers so far and how deep
each has gone, the open numbers, the notes kept, the participant's pace. Judged by inference.
| outcome | when | then |
|---|---|---|
| deeper | an answer has not reached what would have helped | "Why do you think…?" or "What happened next?" on it, naming the number it follows |
| anchor-check | an answer names what helped or would have helped | ask what would have stopped it happening, in their words |
| new-event | a story has reached what helped, or ran dry | ask about a different time or a different kind of task |
| clarify | a "heard" line was a guess | ask which number it was for |
| fewer | replies are getting shorter or slower | one question or none, and say they can stop anytime |
Rules for every question: open, not yes-or-no, never leading, never a skipped number again, at most two follow-ups on
one answer, at most three in all. Precedence: clarify, then deeper, then anchor-check, then new-event. Default:
deeper.

**`note-capture`: does something become a note, and what kind?** Inputs: the answer, the canon's entries and trees
at the pinned commit, the notes already kept. Judged by inference.
| outcome | when | then |
|---|---|---|
| touches-canon | it matches an existing canon entry | propose a note naming that entry in the record (never in the email) |
| candidate | no canon entry fits: a new symptom, cause, root cause or anchor | propose a note marked as a candidate of that kind |
| not-a-note | an opinion with no event behind it, or the same as a kept note | propose nothing |
| withhold | it would identify a person, an employer or a client, and can't be said without it | propose nothing, keep nothing |
Precedence: withhold first. Default: not-a-note.

### A3. `crafts/prompt-therapy/scripts/thread.py` and `tests/test_thread.py` (standard library only)
- `parse(reply)`: the labelled parts, as `(label, text)` pairs, for lines opening `Q<n>` or `N<n>` followed by `:`,
  `.` or a space; the text left over is returned unlabelled.
- `next(used, k)`: the next `k` unused `Q` numbers.
- `check(email, asked, skipped, sent, ceiling)`: the list of problems from step 9; empty means it may be sent.
- The tests use made-up replies only. No real email text is ever a test fixture (`dmc:fixture-source`).

### Falsification of this revision
- "Four records for one email loop is too many": refuted. Each decides a different question with different inputs:
  what to do, how to read, what to ask, what to keep. Folding them makes one record whose outcomes answer four
  questions, which no single precedence can order.
- "Reading the numbers needs no code": refuted by recurrence. It happens on every reply, and a wrong split puts an
  answer under the wrong number; the parser and `check` make it the same every time and testable.
- "The thread as the only state is fragile": survives, scoped. If the thread is lost, so is the conversation, and
  nothing in the repo could rebuild it anyway without copying the participant's words, which the plan forbids. The
  kept notes survive in the record file.
- "Unlabelled answers will be misread": survives, scoped. A guess is always shown in the "heard" line with its number,
  so the participant can correct it, and a two-way guess is asked about instead.
