## Definition of done (proposed, from provenance #11 entries 1–5)
1. An outreach to one adult participant, run by email from the operator's mailbox.
2. Consent captured in the email before any research question, and again for each kept note.
3. A generative interview walking the why-tree, ending when the participant stops responding, says stop, or the
   ceiling is reached.
4. The only output that leaves the mailbox: de-identified records under an HMAC id, each consented, and a list of
   candidate new nodes (symptoms, causes, root causes, anchors) not yet in the canon.

## Intent as read
The operator wants to learn from one real Claude Code user what AI agents do in their work, and whether the canon's
why-trees miss nodes. Advice offered back draws only on the canon, as hypotheses. The operator asked that the session
run as long as possible. The falsification below turns that into: as long as the participant finds it worth their
time.

## Mutation
### A. The decision record and its play-book (craft zone, one PR)
- `crafts/prompt-therapy/playbooks/user-research.md`: Trigger, Steps, Judgements, Questions and Evidence, as every
  play-book has.
- `crafts/prompt-therapy/dmc/research-session.jsonld`, homed there and judged by inference. It decides the agent's next
  act in the conversation. Its outcomes:
  - **invite:** send the operator-approved invitation, then wait.
  - **consent:** before any research question, a clear "I agree" in the thread. If it is declined, or unclear twice,
    thank the participant and end.
  - **explore:** one open question per email walking the tree: what they saw, their own why first, then one level
    deeper.
  - **offer:** when asked for causes or advice, offer canon causes and anchors as questions, labelled as not
    professional advice; never diagnose.
  - **capture:** a node the canon lacks is read back in de-identified words with "May I keep this?", and kept only on
    a yes.
  - **off-scope:** the employer's or clients' confidential work, colleagues by name, family, health, money or legal
    matters. Decline gently, keep nothing, steer back.
  - **escalate:** distress or safety, anything only the operator can answer, any request to send files or code, or
    doubt. Stop and ask the operator; nothing is sent until they answer.
  - **follow-up:** one short nudge after 3 days without a reply.
  - **end:** a "stop", a decline, 7 days without a reply after the follow-up, 20 emails from the agent, 4 weeks, or the
    operator ending it. Thank them, say what was kept, stop.
- `evolve.md` cites it. The record holds the allowed topics, the tone and the metrics below.
- **Allowed topics:** what AI agents (Claude Code included) did in the participant's work, as they experienced it; why
  they think it happened; what it cost them; what they wished it had done.
- **Tone:**
  - plain, warm, curious and brief, at most about 120 words and one question per email;
  - the participant's words, never the corpus's;
  - every email signed "Claude (AI), writing on behalf of your [relation]".
- **Metrics, never length:**
  - new candidate nodes;
  - why-tree levels walked to an anchor;
  - consented notes kept;
  - reply latency, as a fatigue signal;
  - stop signals honoured: 100%.

### B. The participant id (workstation zone, one PR)
- `workstation-corpus/ops/11-h1-participant-id.sh`, an ops script the operator runs. It reads the participant's
  address and a secret key the operator chooses from the terminal, without echo. It prints only
  `HMAC-SHA-256(key, lowercased address)`, cut to 16 hex characters with a `p-` prefix.
- The operator keeps the key in their own private storage and pastes back only the id. The agent never holds the key
  or the mapping.
- It is a read-only host action with one change-log row (operator).

### C. The outreach (the mailbox; no repository change)
- Mail is sent from the operator's connected Gmail (`send_message`, `reply` in the one thread).
- Only that thread is read: `search_threads` for the participant's address, then `get_thread`. No other mail is read.
- Check-ins are scheduled on this session every 6 hours while the thread is open (`send_later`), and cancelled at the
  end.
- After each exchange the operator gets a de-identified summary in the chat: the outcome applied and why.
- **The mandate (Rule-2, mandate scope).**
  - **Rule:** emails whose outcome is consent, explore, offer, capture, off-scope, follow-up or end, in the one thread.
  - **Ceiling:** 20 emails from the agent.
  - Invite is carried by this plan-`Y` only with the text below, exactly. Escalate always returns to the operator.

### D. The records (agent zone, on a branch until #11's Done)
- `agent-corpus/research/<p-id>.md`. It holds:
  - `consent: agreed <date>`;
  - one block per kept note: date, `kept: yes <date>`, the canon node it touches or a candidate new node, in
    de-identified words.

  It never holds the participant's own sentences verbatim, their address, name or relation, or the thread.
- If the participant later asks for deletion, their notes are removed in the next PR. The branch holds them until Done
  to keep git history clean.
- The candidates are a hand-up for dyad-system's canon, once its intake has an observation shape (phase plan gap 1),
  sent only on the operator's words.

## Falsification
- "As long an interview as possible": refuted as the goal. It pushes toward pressure and padding. The session lasts as
  long as the participant finds it worthwhile, and the metrics above measure insight.
- "A hash of his email anonymizes the records": refuted. An unkeyed hash is reversible by anyone who knows or guesses
  the address. The HMAC with a key the agent never holds survives.
- "Consent in the first reply is enough": survives, scoped. Consent to take part is given once; each kept note is
  consented again, and a "stop" ends it at any time.
- "The agent writes from the operator's mailbox, so the participant might think it is the operator": refuted by
  construction. Every email opens and is signed as Claude, an AI.
- "Reading the mailbox reaches beyond the thread": survives, scoped. Only the one thread is searched and read, and
  this is stated to the operator here.
- Nothing here depends on #3, #10 or the paused `specs` work.
