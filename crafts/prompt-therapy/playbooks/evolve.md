# Play-book: evolve (the prompt-therapy craft; d-work #9)

Taking what operation and users teach back into the product: the specifications, the code, or dyad-system's canon.
Lifecycle: `product-development.md`.

## Trigger
A finding from `operate.md`, a test failure classed as a moved canon, a user's complaint, or a candidate a
participant agreed to save in a research interview (`user-research.md`).

## Steps
1. **De-identify** the finding: no name, no client, no employer, no private context. One that cannot be is dropped.
2. **Route it** by `dmc:feedback-route`.
3. **A hand-up** to dyad-system is split item by item by `dmc:intake-split` (the core's) and sent only on the
   operator's words, through the channel the operator names.
4. **Propose** the row the route names; nothing is changed on the finding alone.

## Judgements
| judgement | record |
|---|---|
| where a finding goes | `dmc:feedback-route` |
| how a hand-up is split | `dmc:intake-split` |

## Questions
Each proposed row's plan question; each hand-up's sending, in words.

## Evidence
Each finding, de-identified, with its route and the row or hand-up it became.
